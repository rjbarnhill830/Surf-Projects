Surf spot predictor

Rule-based spot-matching model. Spot profiles are built from published surf-forecast guides; buoy and forecast readings come from live public data. Log real sessions below and the model nudges its scoring toward what's actually worked for you.

Your surfing

Skill level filters out spots that need more than you've got — set it honestly, it's a safety check, not just a label. Wave style is a soft nudge toward what you're in the mood for; leave all unchecked for no preference.

Forecast

Pull a full swell + wind + tide outlook for a day, a few days, or the week ahead, and see which spot(s) are likely to be best and when.

Customize spot profiles

Edit any spot's ideal swell, wind and tide if you know it better than the published guides, or add a spot you haven't surfed yet but want to consider — set its profile from what you've heard works there, with or without any logged sessions. Changes are saved to this browser and used in the ranking below. Spots are listed north to south; click a marker on the map or a card below to view or edit that spot.

Load from your spreadsheet

This tool can't reach files on your computer on its own — browsers don't allow that. Instead, click below and pick your spreadsheet file directly; it's read right here in your browser and never leaves your machine. Do this again any time you've added new rows to the "Surf Tracker" tab.
